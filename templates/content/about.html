About Form D Explorer

Publisher and purpose

Form D Explorer is built and maintained by Dawn Crest Consulting. It organizes public SEC disclosures so researchers can find legal issuers, follow filing histories and compare reported information with its source.

Search covers Form D placements, investment advisers, Regulation Crowdfunding and Regulation A filings. Our research section adds calculations over stored Form D records. Distinct CIKs, dated disclosures and transparent missing values keep those calculations tied to identifiable records.

Data and independence

Sources include SEC EDGAR and Investment Adviser Public Disclosure. Form D Explorer is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of the SEC.

A filing is an issuer's disclosure. It does not establish investment merit, current availability, cash received or a completed financing. Name-based adviser suggestions require verification. Read the methodology and live dataset coverage before using an aggregate.

Contact and corrections

Email luke@dawncrestconsulting.com for product questions, support or data corrections. For a data issue, include the accession number or adviser identifier, source link, affected field and what differs. Do not send confidential investor or account information.

Our editorial standards explain verification and corrections. Account and personal-data requests are described in the privacy policy.

How the service is supported

Public search and research tools are free to use. Paid exports and subscriptions are described on the pricing page. If advertising is enabled, it is labeled and kept separate from research results; advertising does not establish a filing's importance or an issuer's credibility.

Content is general research information, not legal, tax or investment advice.
