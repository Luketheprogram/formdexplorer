Scope and reproducibility

Analytical reports count unique accession numbers with a nonblank stored issuer name and a filing date no later than today. Issuer counts use distinct CIKs. Original notices exclude records classified as amendments by either submission type or the amendment flag. Source records remain available in existing search and filing histories.

Monthly series cover twelve calendar months including the current partial month. Period comparisons use the two preceding completed calendar months. The dataset may still be incomplete in those months. State uses current stored issuer address; industry uses the classification on each filing. These choices can affect historical comparisons.

Offering amount, amount sold and investor count are counted as usable only when stored as a nonnegative number. Missing or invalid negative values are unavailable; a zero is usable. No totals of reported offering or sold-dollar fields are calculated.

Keep the page URL, calculation time, month labels and source accessions in your research log. The data can change as imports and corrections arrive; cached results are not immutable historical snapshots.
