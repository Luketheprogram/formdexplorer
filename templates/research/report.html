Why a naive total fails

In a hypothetical example, an initial notice reports $2 million sold and an amendment for the same offering reports $3 million sold. Adding them produces $5 million, duplicating earlier sales. Even a $1 million difference is not necessarily cash received during the interval: corrections and commitments require checking the documents.

Missing offering amounts may represent an indefinite amount or an extraction limitation. They cannot be used as zero or inferred from other fields. No sold-dollar sum or market-size estimate is produced here.
