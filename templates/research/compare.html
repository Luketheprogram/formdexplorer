Interpretation limits

State uses the issuer's current stored business address, not historical location, investor residence or capital deployment. Industry is issuer-reported on each filing. A CIK may appear in multiple industries and months. Small samples and uneven imports can dominate differences; these figures do not estimate the national market.

Percentage change = (later month − earlier month) ÷ earlier month × 100; unavailable when the earlier count is zero. Dataset share uses all eligible stored Form D accessions in the same month as denominator. Methodology · Coverage. Public data cache for up to 15 minutes.
