Does a reported offering amount prove that amount was raised?

Reveal explanation

No. The offering amount and reported amount sold are different source fields, and neither independently verifies cash received.

Can you add an initial notice and its amendment to calculate proceeds?

Reveal explanation

Not safely. An amendment can repeat cumulative figures. Review the offering relationship and actual changes first.

Do two filings with the same CIK necessarily describe one offering?

Reveal explanation

No. The CIK identifies the filer. Offering relationships need their own supporting evidence.

Does a blank amount equal zero?

Reveal explanation

No. An explicit zero is a numeric disclosure; an unavailable field cannot be treated as a zero observation.

Can monthly distinct issuer counts be added into an annual total?

Reveal explanation

Not without deduplication. One CIK can appear in multiple months.
